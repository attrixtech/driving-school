# MASTER SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# Driving School Management ERP – thrumble.driving-school

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a complete, production-ready Driving School Management ERP for the Indian market.

The software will be used by:
- Private driving schools.
- Motor training institutes.
- Two-wheeler training schools.
- Four-wheeler driving schools.
- Heavy vehicle training institutes.
- Commercial driver training centers.
- Multi-branch driving academies.
- Fleet-based driver training businesses.

The application must manage the complete operational lifecycle of a driving school, from student enquiry and admission to training, instructor allocation, vehicle scheduling, attendance, theory classes, practical lessons, mock driving tests, fee collection, documentation, and student completion.

The product must be developed as a commercial SaaS application under the brand:

**thrumble.driving-school**

The application must not be a basic student management system. It must be a complete business operations platform for driving schools.

Primary objectives:
1. Digitize driving school operations.
2. Reduce manual paperwork.
3. Manage students and their training progress.
4. Prevent instructor and vehicle scheduling conflicts.
5. Track driving lesson hours and performance.
6. Manage student payments and outstanding fees.
7. Maintain vehicle documents and maintenance records.
8. Provide an online admission and enquiry facility.
9. Help school owners monitor revenue, performance, and operations.

# 3. SAAS MULTI-TENANT ARCHITECTURE

The system must support multiple independent driving schools.

Each driving school must have its own:
- Business profile.
- Branches.
- Students.
- Instructors.
- Vehicles.
- Training schedules.
- Admissions.
- Payments.
- Expenses.
- Reports.
- Employees.
- Subscription.

Use a shared database architecture with tenant_id or an equivalent properly implemented tenancy solution.

All tenant-owned records must be isolated.

One driving school must never access another school's:
- Student records.
- Driving license documents.
- Instructor records.
- Vehicle information.
- Payments.
- Financial data.
- Training records.

Support multiple branches under one tenant.

A school owner can view all authorized branches, while branch managers can only access their assigned branches.

Create a separate platform super-admin panel to manage tenants, subscriptions, plans, usage, and platform settings.

### Scheduling Rules

Before confirming a lesson, verify:

1. Student is enrolled and active.
2. Student has sufficient eligible lessons remaining.
3. Instructor is available.
4. Instructor is qualified for the selected vehicle category.
5. Vehicle is available.
6. Vehicle is not under maintenance.
7. Vehicle is not assigned to another lesson during the same period.
8. Instructor is not assigned to another lesson.
9. Student has no overlapping lesson.
10. Branch and operating-hour rules are satisfied.

Prevent scheduling conflicts using server-side validation and appropriate database locking or transaction handling.
